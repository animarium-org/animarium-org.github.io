Lemma 5.84. (Key lemma, [Anel et al. 2025, Lemmas 2.3.27 and 2.3.28])

Let \(L\) be an acyclic class.

  1. \(\Delta \nabla(L) \subseteq L\).

  2. \(\Delta^{-1}(L) \cap \EffEpi \subseteq \EffEpi \cdot L\).

Proof
(1) For \(u\colon A \to B\) in \(L\), the morphism \(\Delta \nabla(u)\) takes the form
\[\Delta \nabla(u)\colon B \sqcup_A B \longrightarrow (B \sqcup_A B) \times_B (B \sqcup_A B).\]
The two summand inclusions \(i_k\colon B \to B \sqcup_A B\) induce two maps \(i_k \times \id \colon B \times_B (B \sqcup_A B) \to (B \sqcup_A B) \times_B (B \sqcup_A B)\) which are jointly an effective epimorphism. Since \(L\) is local, it suffices to show that the left vertical map in the following pullback square is in \(L\): This holds since \(i_k\) is a cobase change of \(u\).
(2) Let \(u\colon A\to B\) be an effective epimorphism with \(\Delta_u\in L\). Write \(C_m=A^{\times_B(m+1)}\) for its Čech nerve. Each degeneracy repeats a coordinate and is a base change of \(\Delta_u\). For \(m>0\) the latching map \(L_m C\to C_m\) is the colimit in \(\Ar(T)\) of the composites of proper degeneracies into \(C_m\). Indeed the indexing poset of proper surjections from \([m]\) is the poset of nonempty sets of adjacent coordinates identified, with the reverse inclusion order; it has an initial object identifying all coordinates. Hence the constant diagram of targets has colimit \(C_m\). Each degeneracy composite belongs to \(L\) and \(L\) is closed under colimits, so the latching map belongs to \(L\).
The passage from the \((m-1)\)-skeleton to the \(m\)-skeleton of the realization attaches the \(m\)-simplex by a cobase change of the pushout product of this latching map with \(\partial\Delta^m\to\Delta^m\). Here the simplicial sets denote their animae carried into \(T\) by the constant-object functor. The boundary is nonempty for \(m>0\), so this second morphism is an effective epimorphism. Every attachment is therefore in \(\EffEpi\cdot L\). This class is closed under composition and colimits in \(\Ar(T)\), and the sequential composite is \(C_0\to\abs{C_\bullet}\), which is \(u\) by effectiveness.

References

  1. Mathieu Anel, Georg Biedermann, Eric Finster, André Joyal. Left-exact localizations of $\infty$-topoi III: The acyclic product. 2025.
