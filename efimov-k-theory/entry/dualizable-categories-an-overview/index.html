Theorem 1.2 (Lurie, [Lurie (2018), Proposition D.7.3.1]). Consider \(C \in \PrLst \). The following are equivalent:

(1)

There exists a localization \(\phi \colon D \to E\) in \(\PrL _{\omega ,\st }\) such that \(C = \ker (\phi )\);

(2)

\(C\) is dualizable in \((\PrLst ,\otimes )\);

(3)

The functor \(\colim \colon \Ind (C) \to C\) has a left adjoint (“compactly assembled”);

(4)

\(C\) is a retract in \(\PrLst \) of a compactly generated category;

(5)

(Efimov, [Efimov (2024), Theorem 5.2]) \(C \otimes (-)\colon \PrLst \to \PrLst \) preserves fully faithful functors (“flat”);

(6)

(Clausen–Scholze, see [Efimov (2024), Proposition 2.51]) arbitrary products distribute over filtered colimits (“AB6”).
