Proposition A.30. Let \(C_0\) be a small category with finite colimits and let \(D\) be a presentable category. There is an equivalence, natural in \(D\), \[ \Ind (C_0) \otimes D \simeq \Fun ^{\lex }(C_0\catop ,D), \] where the right-hand side denotes the full subcategory of \(\Fun (C_0\catop ,D)\) spanned by the functors preserving finite limits. If \(C_0\) and \(D\) are stable, this gives natural equivalences \[ \Ind (C_0) \otimes D \simeq \Fun ^{\ex }(C_0\catop ,D) \simeq \Fun ^{\colim }(\Ind (C_0\catop ),D). \] Taking \(D=\An \) recovers the description of \(\Ind (C_0)\) by left exact presheaves in Synthetic Category Theory, Remark 18.2.7.

Proof.

By Theorem A.28, we have \[ \Ind (C_0) \otimes D \simeq \Fun ^{\lim }(\Ind (C_0)\catop ,D) \simeq \Fun ^{\colim }(\Ind (C_0),D\catop )\catop . \] Since \(D\) admits small limits, \(D\catop \) admits small colimits, so by Theorem A.15(3) restriction along \(y\) gives an equivalence \[ \Fun ^{\colim }(\Ind (C_0),D\catop ) \simeq \Fun ^{\rex }(C_0,D\catop ) = \Fun ^{\lex }(C_0\catop ,D)\catop . \] This proves the first claim. In the stable case, a functor between stable categories preserves finite limits if and only if it is exact, and applying Theorem A.15(3) to \(C_0\catop \) (which again has finite colimits) and the cocomplete category \(D\) gives \(\Fun ^{\ex }(C_0\catop ,D) \simeq \Fun ^{\colim }(\Ind (C_0\catop ),D)\). □
