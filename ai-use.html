AI use

AI assistance has been used to develop the tools that turn the LaTeX manuscripts into web editions. The manuscripts are the source of the mathematical text.

For the stable homotopy theory book, Bastiaan Cnossen used AI agents to proofread, suggest improvements to the exposition, and sometimes draft mathematical material. In preparing lecture notes, he has also used AI to turn live notes into LaTeX, fill in proofs, and look up citations. He has used AI for editing the synthetic category theory manuscript.

See each work’s own declaration for details, and Bastiaan’s personal statement for more on his research practice.
