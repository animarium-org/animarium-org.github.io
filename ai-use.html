AI use

Animarium was built with AI assistance. The mathematical content is taken from LaTeX manuscripts and gets converted into web editions using AI-written conversion scripts.

Generative AI has also been used in various forms in the preparation of the LaTeX manuscripts. For the stable homotopy theory book, AI agents were used to proofread, suggest improvements to the exposition, and sometimes draft mathematical material. See the book’s declaration on AI use for details. For the lecture notes, AI was used to turn live notes from the lectures into LaTeX, fill in details of proofs not captured in the live notes, and look up citations. For the synthetic category theory manuscript, AI was used for editing and helped formalize material from the opening chapters.

See also Bastiaan Cnossen’s statement on AI use.
