About Animarium

Animarium is an experiment in publishing mathematical books and lecture notes as an interlinked library. You can read a work chapter by chapter, follow a reference directly to an individual result, or download the full manuscript as a PDF. The web editions and the ways they link to one another are still developing.

To cite an individual result, use its tag link, such as animarium.org/tag/1000/. The tag stays with the result if it moves to another chapter or work.

Animarium is maintained by Bastiaan Cnossen. If you spot an error or a broken link, please include the page address or tag when you get in touch. For more on how the web editions are produced, see AI use.
